# ConditionalTwin finite development selection protocol

Component predictions are generated by strict leave-one-host-out cross-fitting: every scored record is predicted by a component model fitted without that record's host.

The full development selector scores the eight fixed combinations below on these cross-fitted predictions. The 30 host-row-exclusion tables are sensitivity analyses that omit one host's rows and re-score the same fixed cross-fitted predictions; they do not refit all components after the designated host is removed and are therefore not nested outer-fold model-selection estimates.

## Selection rule

1. Retain candidates within 0.005 of the best pair-weighted within-host AUROC.
2. Among those, retain candidates within 0.010 of the best host-centered AUROC.
3. Order survivors by pooled AUROC (descending), macro-host AUROC (descending), then sum of absolute branch weights (ascending).

## Weighting and final fitting

- Base ExtraTrees: inverse host-frequency sample weights normalized to mean one, plus class_weight=balanced.
- Conditional ExtraTrees: equal aggregate weight per host; mixed-class hosts allocate half of host weight to each class, single-class hosts distribute host weight uniformly; weights normalized to mean one; class_weight=None.
- Five fixed seeds: 20260715-20260719. After development selection, final component ensembles are fitted on all 529 development records and seed probabilities are averaged.
